# Code of Conduct

DeliveryGuard is a collaborative technical project. Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss behavior and evidence rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and in good faith.
